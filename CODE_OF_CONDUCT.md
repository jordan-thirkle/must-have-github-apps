# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community. Participate in good faith, assume context may be missing, and focus criticism on ideas and evidence rather than people.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, deliberate misinformation, and unwanted commercial promotion.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled in confidence where possible. Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate this standard and may restrict participation when necessary.
